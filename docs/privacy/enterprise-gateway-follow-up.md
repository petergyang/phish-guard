# Enterprise Gateway Follow-Up

## Why It Is Deferred

Enterprise gateways are powerful but require admin deployment, mail-routing integration, and organizational policy decisions. That is not the consumer Gmail MVP.

## Choose This When

- The target customer is an organization rather than an individual.
- The product needs pre-delivery enforcement.
- Admins need reporting, policy, or quarantine workflows.

## Required Constraints

- Preserve user-visible evidence where warnings reach end users.
- Define false-positive review and override flows.
- Integrate with existing mail authentication signals.
- Keep personal inbox content out of product analytics unless explicitly required and approved.
