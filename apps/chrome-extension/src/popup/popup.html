Gmail Phish Guard

Shows a warning inside Gmail when a sender looks like it may be pretending to be a trusted brand.

  • Looks at the sender row only.
  • Does not read message bodies, links, or attachments.
  • Does not upload email data.
Gmail access is off. Turn it on to show warnings inside opened emails.

Chrome will ask for access to mail.google.com because the warning has to appear inside Gmail.
